# Embryo Instant Search

Embryo Instant Search is Embryo-Zero's fork of [typesense](https://github.com/typesense/typesense) (8532fc2, commit 8532fc2). It does typo-tolerant instant search.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE.txt) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
